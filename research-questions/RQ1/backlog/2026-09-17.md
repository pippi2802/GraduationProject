## Real bugs found and fixed in the notebook

1. **`PERIOD_US` used before definition** — cell 8 referenced it, but it was only
   defined in cell 12, further down. Only worked due to leftover kernel state from an
   out-of-order run; would `NameError` on a clean restart. This was flagged as the
   likely explanation for several "results don't match expectations" moments earlier
   in the day — the notebook wasn't guaranteed to reflect one consistent run.
2. **`07_worst_case.sh` never touches CPU frequency pinning** — confirmed via `grep`,
   zero mentions of governor/turbo in the script. So `07` ("worst case, everything
   removed") was actually running with frequency still pinned to `performance` the
   whole time — not truly "everything removed." Decided not to fix/recollect for
   today; `07` dropped from the presented slide deck instead (see below).
3. **Cell 14 (old overlapping-hue boxplot) left in place alongside cell 15 (new
   faceted 2×N version)** — redundant, cell 14 should be deleted.
4. Confirmed (not a bug, a real finding): `worst_case`'s mid-pack tornado ranking at
   U=0.7 is genuine, not an artifact — its own U=0.7 box in the faceted boxplot looks
   unexceptional next to its peers. The real caveat is that `07`'s own ceiling (0.79)
   sits much closer to the U=0.7 comparison point than the other scenarios' ceiling
   (0.94) does, so the tornado at U=0.7 isn't comparing everyone at equal remaining
   margin.

  U=0.7 where the confound is less consequential.
