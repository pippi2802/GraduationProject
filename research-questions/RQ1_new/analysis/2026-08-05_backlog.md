# RQ1_new — 2026-08-05 backlog

## What was found

Three distinct measurement-reliability bugs were identified and fixed in
`run_job.sh`, all variations of the same root problem: a co-runner (model2's
neighbour, or model3's competitor/interferer) reporting `Ready` without
anyone verifying it was actually consuming CPU. `Ready` only confirms the
container process started, not that it holds real scheduling bandwidth.

1. **Model3-w2 (sibling, reserved competitor), round3**: 7 of 10 tight-scale
   cells were silently collected under the wrong condition
   (`COMPETITOR_TYPE=unreserved` instead of `reserved`), traced to a manual
   rerun of previously-failed cells that didn't set the env var. Fixed by
   rerunning with the correct value explicitly set.
2. **Reserved competitor (model3-w2/w4)**: no check beyond `Ready` existed at
   placement, mid-scale restart, or per-cell. Added `confirm_burning_cpu()` —
   samples a pod's actual CPU time twice, a few seconds apart, and requires
   it to have advanced — at all three points, with retry on failure.
3. **Unreserved interferer (model3 base)**: had *no* liveness check at all.
   Same fix applied. This explains why every existing round of the base
   model3 arm shows a coin-flip pattern (`miss_rate` randomly 0% or ~100% at
   the same U across rounds) — the interferer's actual liveness was never
   verified.
4. **Separately**: a `continue` statement inside `run_one_cell()` (a bash
   function, not a loop) was a no-op — bash printed an error and fell
   through, meaning cells that failed the calibration-cv gate were being
   collected anyway instead of skipped. Fixed (`continue` → `return`).

None of these are properties of Azure/cloud infrastructure — they are gaps
in what the harness verified before trusting a cell's data.

## One genuine infrastructure-level finding (not a bug)

An isolated 91-consecutive-miss burst in model3-w4 (physical placement,
otherwise clean) was traced to a real kernel-level RT-bandwidth throttling
event — not competitor contention (ruled out: same pattern appears in the
solo baseline with no co-runner at all). Rare (well under 1% of jobs across
every clean arm), consistent in magnitude with the reservation's own budget
enforcement occasionally engaging. Documented in `w4_burst_note.md`,
retained in the dataset rather than excluded since it reflects a real system
behavior, not a measurement artifact.
